# Public Website, Help, and Entry Pages

Back to the [manual index](./README.md).

## Public Pages

Legal WatchDog also includes public-facing pages outside the authenticated app. These pages are useful for new users, prospects, and invited guests.

The public site includes pages such as:

- Home
- Features
- How It Works
- About Us
- Contact Us
- FAQ
- Help Center
- Privacy Policy
- Terms
- Demo or squeeze page style entry points

## What These Pages Are For

Use the public pages when you need to:

- understand the product before signing in
- compare the product to your monitoring needs
- review legal terms and privacy information
- contact the team
- share basic product information with colleagues

## Help Center

The Help Center is the natural first stop for:

- general product orientation
- common questions
- getting unstuck before contacting support

## FAQ

The FAQ page is useful for fast answers about:

- what the platform does
- who it is for
- how monitoring works at a high level
- basic usage concerns

## Contact Us

Use the contact page when:

- you need direct support
- you want to ask about a feature or account issue
- you want to request a demo or business conversation

## Guest Access

The app includes a guest access entry point. This is helpful for controlled access scenarios where someone may need to view shared material without full workspace membership.

In practice, guest access is most relevant when a user is invited into a narrow workflow such as a ticket or review context.

## Onboarding Page

The public onboarding page is designed to orient first-time users before or around account setup. It should be seen as a bridge between the marketing site and the full application.

## Best Way to Use the Public Site

- If you are evaluating the product, start with Home, Features, and How It Works.
- If you need policy or legal information, open Terms and Privacy Policy.
- If you need direct assistance, use Help Center first and Contact Us second.
