# Getting Started

Back to the [manual index](./README.md).

## What Legal WatchDog Does

Legal WatchDog helps teams monitor external websites for legal, regulatory, and policy changes. Instead of checking many sites manually, you can group your work in the app and let the platform organize monitoring around:

- organizations
- projects
- jurisdictions
- sources
- revisions and tickets

## Who Should Use It

This app is suited to:

- compliance teams
- in-house legal teams
- law firms
- immigration and visa specialists
- consultants who manage monitoring for multiple clients

## Main Concepts

Before using the app, it helps to understand the core terms:

- Organization: your company, client, department, or workspace container.
- Project: a monitoring initiative inside an organization.
- Jurisdiction: the legal, regulatory, policy, or geographic scope you want to monitor.
- Source: a website or page that Legal WatchDog checks for changes.
- Revision: a detected version or update from a source.
- Ticket: a follow-up work item created from a detected change.

## Sign Up

The sign-up flow asks for:

- full name
- work email
- password
- password confirmation

Password validation is strict. Your password must include:

- at least 8 characters
- an uppercase letter
- a lowercase letter
- a number
- a special character

After sign-up, you are typically sent to OTP verification.

## Log In

The login screen supports:

- company email and password
- a remember-me option
- Google sign-in

If you arrive with a pending invitation, the app can prompt you to accept that invitation after login.

## OTP and Verification

The app includes OTP flows for account verification. If your account is pending verification, follow the prompts until your identity and email are confirmed.

## Create an Organization During Onboarding

Some users are sent directly into organization setup after authentication. This screen asks for:

- company name
- work email
- job title
- industry
- organization type
- company size
- country

Once this step is complete, the app tries to take you into the organization workspace. In some approval-based cases, you may instead land on an account pending approval page.

## Accepting an Invitation

If someone invites you into an organization:

1. Open the invitation link.
2. If you are signed in, confirm the invite.
3. If you are not signed in, the app sends you to login first.
4. After acceptance, you are usually taken to the organization area.

## First Things to Do After Login

For most new users, the recommended setup order is:

1. Open or create an organization.
2. Create your first project.
3. Add a jurisdiction.
4. Save instructions for that jurisdiction.
5. Add sources.
6. Start monitoring.
